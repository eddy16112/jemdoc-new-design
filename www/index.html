A few common questions about getting your network project started.

Do you work with homes and small businesses?

Yes. BaySpan Networks provides network design, installation, and setup for residential customers and small businesses in the San Francisco Bay Area.

What should I have ready for the first conversation?

Your city, the type of space, the equipment you already have, and a short description of what you want to connect or improve are a helpful starting point. You don’t need a finished network plan.

Can we discuss the equipment I already own?

Absolutely. Tell us what you currently use and what you need from the network. We can discuss how your existing equipment fits into the proposed setup before deciding what the project needs.

How do I get started?

Call (347) 949-8934 to discuss your space and network needs. We’ll start with the details of your project and work through the next steps together.
